Real-Time Resource Tracking

Hospitals update resource availability dynamically (e.g., beds, ICU, specialists).Users can view live updates on the availability of critical services.

Search and Filter

Users can search for hospitals by location, specialty, or resource type. Filters allow customization (e.g., cost, distance, or emergency services).

Emergency Alerts

An emergency tab for critical needs that shows prioritized nearby hospitals. Option for users to notify hospitals of their arrival, reducing wait times. Allows users to send automated alerts to family, emergency contacts, or healthcare providers with their location and medical status.
